Privacy policy and GDPR information

1. Operator and contact

Scandoc is operated by Ondřej Novotný, who can be contacted about privacy at info@vyvojari-uo.cz. The operator is the controller of personal data received through support correspondence. The operator does not receive the contents of your locally stored documents and has no remote access to them.

2. Data used by the app

  • Documents: page images, document names, creation dates and page order. These are processed for scanning, cropping, history and export and are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Settings: your selected language is stored locally in system preferences.
  • Attachments: PDF and JPG exports for sharing are temporary files in the device cache.
  • Support: when you contact the operator, your email address, message and any information you choose to attach are processed.

Scandoc has no advertising network or separate app analytics service operated by its developer. It does not sell document contents. This does not mean that system services, Google Play Services or this website's hosting provider process no technical data; these are described below.

3. Camera and system scanning

On iOS, the app uses Apple VisionKit and the system camera permission. On Android, it uses ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play Services; that service provides camera access and may offer gallery import. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

According to Google's documentation, image processing for document scanning takes place on your device. Google states that ML Kit does not send input images or processing results to Google servers. Android may require an internet connection to download scanner components.

Google also describes technical data collection by ML Kit, including device and app information, installation or device identifiers, performance information and error events for diagnostics and usage analysis. The scope depends on the service and version. These services are subject to Google's privacy policy; Apple system services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Sharing, email and backups

You initiate sharing or email yourself. Scandoc hands the selected files to the app you choose; email opens a draft and you confirm sending in your email client. The Scandoc operator is not a recipient of those files. After handoff, data is handled under the rules of the destination app and its provider, such as Google Drive, iCloud or your email service.

Scandoc provides no cloud synchronisation of its own. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled. On iOS, system backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Exports and backup copies may exist outside Scandoc.

5. Purposes, legal bases and retention

Local documents and settings serve the functions you use in the app; the operator does not build a central database from them. Information you provide to support is used to answer your enquiry, resolve problems and protect legal rights. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in providing support under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; where support fulfils a contractual obligation to you, Article 6(1)(b), or a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c), applies.

Ordinary support correspondence is kept for no more than 12 months after resolution, unless further retention is necessary for a specific legal obligation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Send only the information needed to resolve your enquiry. In particular, avoid sending sensitive documents or access credentials.

Documents on your device remain until you remove them. Temporary exports may remain in the cache after deleting a document, until the system removes them or you clear the app's data. Retention by Google, Apple, GitHub or services you share with is governed by those providers' policies.

6. Recipients, security and international transfers

The operator has no access to your local app storage. Email providers may process support messages to deliver and retain them. Information may be disclosed to a public authority to the extent required by law. Transfers and any processing outside the EEA by Google, Apple, GitHub or your chosen providers are described in their policies, including their applicable safeguards.

Local files are protected by the app's private storage and operating-system security. Scandoc does not add its own document encryption or guarantee protection against device loss. Use a device lock and check whom you share files with.

7. Your rights

To the extent that the operator processes your personal data, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Send requests to the contact email; we respond within GDPR time limits, normally within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection or the authority where you live. The operator does not carry out automated decision-making or user profiling. The operator cannot supply document contents that exist only on your device and have never been sent to them.

8. Privacy on this website

This website has no app-operator analytics, advertising pixels, forms, cookies or local tracking storage. Styles, icons and content are served from this site; no external fonts are used. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and may process other technical information under its privacy statement. The Scandoc operator has no access to those server logs.

9. Changes

This policy describes the current app behaviour set out above. We will update it when features or providers change and publish a new effective date. App Store and Google Play data disclosures must match the app version being distributed.

Data and account deletion

Scandoc does not create user accounts, so there is no account to close.

  • Delete a document by opening its detail, choosing Delete and confirming.
  • Remove all local data and settings by clearing app data on Android or deleting the app on iOS. Offloading an iOS app may retain its data.
  • Delete exports, sent attachments, copies in other apps and system backups separately with their respective providers.
  • Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing info@vyvojari-uo.cz; legal exceptions to erasure still apply.

Terms of use

Scandoc is a document scanning and export tool. Use it only for documents you are entitled to process and share, and respect other people's personal data and rights.

Automatic edge detection, cropping and exports may contain errors. Check completeness, readability and page order before sharing or relying on a scan. The app is not a qualified electronic-signature service and does not guarantee the legal validity of a copy. Retain important originals and your own backups.

On Android, scanning requires supported Google Play Services; scanning and sharing availability depend on your device and installed apps. Scandoc does not guarantee the availability of third-party features. Component licences are available in the app under Settings → Third-party licences; their applicable terms remain in effect.

The app is provided as available. This statement does not restrict statutory rights, consumer rights or liability that cannot be excluded under applicable law. The relevant store terms also apply when downloading from the App Store or Google Play. Using the app or this website does not by itself constitute consent to tracking or optional personal-data processing.
